## Shadow Mode surface (shared convention)

The area an agent owns in the background becomes `--o-shadow-bg` with `--o-shadow-fg` text, sky (`--o-shadow-accent`) icons and a 2px sky line on its top edge (`box-shadow: inset 0 2px 0 var(--o-shadow-accent)`). It is a change of room, not a badge. Focus ring on navy is sky. Entering animates over 320ms with the enter easing.
